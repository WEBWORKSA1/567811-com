What you're paying for

Choreography fees usually combine creation (designing each routine), teaching (rehearsal sessions), and sometimes day-of support (cueing, warm-up, blocking at the venue). Music editing and travel can be extra.

What pushes the price up

  • Number of routines and dancers
  • Short timelines (under 3 weeks)
  • Peak wedding season dates
  • Celebrity or competition-level choreographers
  • Travel to venue / destination weddings

How to save without cutting quality

  • Combine families into one medley instead of five separate songs.
  • Book early — 6–8 weeks gives more choice.
  • Use online rehearsals for out-of-town relatives.
  • Share a clear brief: songs, group size, skill level, venue.
