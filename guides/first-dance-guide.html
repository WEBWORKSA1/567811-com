Wedding first dance: the complete guide

Choosing a song, style and teacher — plus how many lessons you'll actually need.

Pick the song first

Choose a song you both love and trim it to 2–3 minutes. Slow songs (60–80 BPM) suit waltz or a simple sway; mid-tempo (90–120 BPM) opens up rumba, bachata or a surprise switch-up.

Choose a style

  • Classic: waltz, foxtrot, rumba
  • Latin warmth: bachata, salsa
  • Surprise mash-up: slow open → upbeat medley

How many lessons?

Complete beginners typically need 4–6 private lessons for a simple routine; 8–10 for lifts, dips or a mash-up. Book the first lesson 6–10 weeks out.

Practise at home

Film each lesson, practise 15 minutes a few times a week with our metronome, and rehearse once in your actual shoes.

On the day

Mark the floor space, test the sound system, and agree a recovery plan (just keep swaying) if anything slips.
