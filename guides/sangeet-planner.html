The 6-week sangeet performance planner

A week-by-week plan to take a family from “we can't dance” to a standing ovation.

Week 6: plan & book

  • Decide performances and who's in each.
  • Shortlist songs; aim for 2–3 minutes per act.
  • Book your choreographer (get matched).

Week 5: music & structure

Lock the edited track. The choreographer maps it into 8-counts and plans entries, formations and exits.

Week 4: learn the bones

Two sessions: learn the main phrases slowly. Record everything on phones and practise with the metronome at a lower BPM.

Free · no obligation

Get up to 3 quotes from matched choreographers

Week 3: full run-throughs

Connect sections, fix formations, add expressions. Out-of-town family can join online.

Week 2: polish

Clean timing, add props or costume changes, rehearse transitions between acts.

Week 1: dress rehearsal

Run the full show in costume with cue sheets, ideally at or near the venue. Plan a warm-up slot on the day.
