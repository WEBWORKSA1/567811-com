How to choose a dance studio or teacher

A 10-point checklist for picking the right studio, class or private teacher.

Know your goal

Fitness, social dancing, a performance or a pro career each need a different kind of teacher. Take the Style Quiz if unsure.

The 10-point checklist

  1. Teacher's training and performance background
  2. Clear level structure (beginner → advanced)
  3. Class size
  4. Trial or drop-in class available
  5. Sprung or suitable floor
  6. Transparent pricing and cancellation policy
  7. Safe, welcoming culture (ask current students)
  8. Child-safety policies for kids' classes
  9. Performance or recital opportunities
  10. Location and schedule you'll actually keep

Red flags

  • Long contracts before a trial
  • No visible pricing
  • Pressure to buy costumes or extras early

Online vs in person

Online is great for learning choreography and drilling at your pace; in-person gives real-time correction and partner work. Many dancers combine both.
