11 dance styles explained

A plain-English guide to the 11 most popular dance styles — and how to choose yours.

Street & urban

Hip Hop

Street style built on grooves (bounce, rock), with sub-styles like popping, locking and breaking. Typical tempo 85–100 BPM.

K-Pop

Synchronised group choreography from Korean pop music videos; precise and formation-heavy.

Stage & studio

Contemporary

Blends modern, jazz and ballet with floor work and release technique; emotion-led.

Ballet

Classical technique — turnout, posture, precision — and the foundation for many styles.

Jazz

Energetic and showy: kicks, turns, leaps, isolations. Staple of musical theatre.

Partner & social

Salsa

Caribbean-rooted partner dance with quick footwork and spins, often counted in 8s at high tempos.

Bachata

Dominican partner dance with a 4-step basic and hip tap; smooth and beginner-friendly.

Ballroom

Partner dances in closed frame — waltz, foxtrot, quickstep — plus Latin ballroom like rumba and cha-cha.

Celebration & cultural

Bollywood

A theatrical fusion of Indian classical, folk and Western moves, built for storytelling and celebration.

Bhangra

Energetic Punjabi folk dance driven by the dhol; big jumps, shoulder bounces and joy.
