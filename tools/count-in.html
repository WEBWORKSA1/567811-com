What BPM is my song?

Tap along with the beat 4–8 times and the metronome locks to it. Most pop sits at 100–130 BPM, hip hop at 85–100, Bollywood dance tracks at 95–130 and salsa at 160–220 (dancers often count salsa in “slow” 8s).

Why accent 1 and 5?

An 8-count is two bars of 4/4. Beat 1 starts the phrase and beat 5 starts its second half — accenting both keeps you oriented, exactly how a teacher counts out loud.
