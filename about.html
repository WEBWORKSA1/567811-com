About

Built on the count

567811 exists for one reason: to get more people dancing — and to connect them with the pros who make it happen.

Every dancer on earth knows the call: “5, 6, 7, 8!” It's the moment before the music hits the one — the half-second where everyone breathes together. We named the site after it.

What we do

  • Learn: free tutorials across 11 styles, organised by level.
  • Practise: free tools — metronome, 8-count sheet, budget estimator, style quiz.
  • Hire: Get Matched connects you with up to 3 choreographers, studios or teachers.
  • Compete: the monthly 11-Count Challenge.

How we make money

Advertising, sponsorships, pro listings and referral fees from professionals — never by charging dancers to request quotes. Supporters help fund prizes and new talent. See disclosures.
