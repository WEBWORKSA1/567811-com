11 styles, one language: the 8-count

Pick a style, follow the level ladder, practise with the metronome. Every tutorial is free.

Level 1

Brand New

Count to 8, find the downbeat, 3 basic steps.

Level 2

Beginner

Combine steps into full 8-counts at 80–100 BPM.

Level 3

Intermediate

Full routines, musicality, turns, formations.

Level 4

Advanced

Freestyle, choreography creation, performance quality.
