FAQ

What does 567811 mean?

It's the dancer's count-in. Choreographers shout “5, 6, 7, 8!” — the last four beats of an 8-count — so everyone starts together on “1”. 567811 = count in, then hit the one.

Is Get Matched really free?

Yes. Requesting quotes is free and there's no obligation to hire. We share your request only with up to 3 relevant choreographers, studios or teachers.

Which countries do you cover?

Requests are welcome worldwide. We're building the strongest networks first in India, the US, Canada, the UK, the UAE and Australia.

How much does a wedding or sangeet choreographer cost?

It depends on city, number of routines, dancers and rehearsals. Use our free Dance Budget Estimator for an indicative range, then request real quotes.

I'm a choreographer / studio. How do I get clients?

Apply on the For Pros page. Founding members get a free listing and first access to matched leads in their city and style.

Do I need experience to use the tutorials?

No. Every style starts at Brand New. Use the Count-In Metronome to practise at a slower BPM and build up.
