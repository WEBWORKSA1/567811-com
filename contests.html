Monthly contest

The 11-Count Challenge

One routine. Eleven counts of pure you. Film it, post it, win it.

Week 1

Theme drops

New theme + beat on the 1st of each month.

Weeks 2–3

Film & submit

Post publicly (YouTube, Instagram or TikTok) with #567811challenge, then submit the link below.

Week 4

Judging & winners

Panel scores + community vote. Winners announced on the last day.

Judging criteria

CriterionWeight
Timing & count accuracy30%
Musicality25%
Creativity25%
Energy & performance20%

Prizes

🥇 Featured creator spotlight + sponsor prize package · 🥈 1-on-1 feedback session with a pro choreographer · 🥉 567811 merch & shout-out. Prize values are announced with each month's theme.

Key rules

  • Open worldwide; entrants under 18 need a parent/guardian's permission to enter.
  • Solo or crew (up to 11 dancers).
  • Original choreography or credited routine; music must be licensed for the platform you post on.
  • One entry per person/crew per month. No purchase necessary.
  • By entering you allow 567811 to embed/link your public video.
  • Full terms in Terms → Contests. Void where prohibited.
