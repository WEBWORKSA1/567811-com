Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and your choices. Last updated 2026-10-08.

What we collect

  • Form data you submit — e.g. name, email, phone, city, event details, budget, messages, video links.
  • Technical data — page visited, referrer and campaign (UTM) tags attached to submissions; standard server logs kept by our host.
  • Browser storage — tool settings (e.g. BPM, choreo sheet) are stored only in your browser.

How we use it

  • To respond to you and to match your request with up to 3 relevant professionals (Get Matched).
  • To review pro applications, auditions, contest entries and job applications.
  • To send newsletters you opt into (unsubscribe anytime).

Sharing

Get Matched requests are shared only with matched professionals, and only with your consent at submission. Forms are delivered through a form-processing service (FormSubmit). We do not sell personal data lists.

Advertising & cookies

We may use Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. If analytics is enabled we use Google Analytics 4.

YouTube

Embedded videos load from youtube-nocookie.com only when you press play. YouTube's own privacy policy then applies.

Retention

Lead and inquiry data is retained for up to 24 months unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data via the contact form (topic: “Privacy request”). Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, PIPEDA/Québec Law 25, CCPA, India's DPDP Act) you may have additional rights.

Children

Get Matched is intended for adults. Under-18s may enter contests only with a parent/guardian's permission.
